General Guidelines

  • No unrealistic accessories or hair (no exaggerated or fantasy elements).
  • Field agents must wear field uniforms suitable for active duty.
  • Skin tones should be realistic (no neon or unnatural colors).
  • Accessories are limited to:
    • Glasses (functional or standard-issue)
    • Vests (tactical or protective)
  • No excessive jewelry or decorative items to maintain professionalism.

For Field Agents

  • Standard field uniform (e.g., dark tactical pants and white shirt, badge visible)
  • Utility vest over the uniform
  • Glasses if needed
  • No hats or unrealistic headgear unless authorized (e.g., helmets for specific operations)

For Office/Administrative Staff

  • Professional attire (e.g., formal)
  • No masks, exaggerated accessories, or unrealistic clothing

Additional Notes

  • All clothing should be clean, well-fitted, and reflect the serious nature of DOJ work.
  • Ensure all team members present a professional and realistic appearance appropriate for law enforcement.

Animations

  • You are not allowed to have any animations other than the rthro animation & toy animation.
